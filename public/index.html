BEFORE YOU BEGIN

You do not have to become
someone else to begin.

Welcome. This is a place to notice, learn, practice, and make choices that fit your life. You can use a fictional example, leave a response blank, repeat a lesson, or take more than sixty calendar days. Completing a lesson means you reviewed it; it never requires sharing personal history.

This is general education, not medical advice, diagnosis, or therapy. “Mental makeover” is a title, not a promise of recovery. If an exercise feels uncomfortable, stop or choose another. You can ask a trusted adult or qualified professional for support at any point.

How this workbook works
  1. Read the objective and explanation.
  2. Study the example and visual model.
  3. Try the guided practice or use a fictional situation.
  4. Check your understanding and reflect in as much detail as you choose.
  5. Mark the lesson reviewed, then decide your next step.

Suggested rhythm: 3 minutes reading, 3 minutes reviewing the example, 5–10 minutes practicing, and a few minutes reflecting. Adapt the time to your access needs and energy.

SUPPORT & PRIVACY

You may pause.
You may ask for help.

This workbook is general education, not therapy, medical advice, diagnosis, or a crisis service. It is not monitored. You never have to disclose trauma or complete an uncomfortable exercise.

If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States and its territories, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline offers crisis support: 988 or https://988lifeline.org/chat/. Elsewhere, use verified local services. Do not wait for a workbook response.

For ongoing concerns, a trusted adult or qualified mental-health professional can help you consider next steps. Facilitators must follow their organization's approved safeguarding and emergency procedures.
